the problem

You pay for leads. Then they reach voicemail.

Most service businesses do not have a lead problem. They have a response problem. The jobs are calling; nobody answers fast enough.

missed calls

The caller does not leave a voicemail

They call the next company on the list. The ad money that produced that call is already spent, and there is no record the lead ever existed.

slow web leads

Website forms sit unanswered for hours

A lead that gets a response in minutes behaves very differently from one that waits until tomorrow. Speed decides who wins the job.

no follow-up

Estimates go out and nothing comes back

Quotes sit in inboxes with no reminder, no follow-up and no owner. Deals that were close to won quietly expire.

scattered systems

Phone, calendar and CRM do not talk

Customer information lives in texts, sticky notes and three different apps. Nobody can see what actually happened with a lead.

questions

Straight answers.

Do I have to replace my current phone system or CRM?
No. The system connects to what you already use where an integration is available. Part of the audit is confirming what your current tools support before we propose anything.
Will customers know they are texting an automated system?
Yes. The response identifies your business, behaves politely and honestly, and hands off to a real person the moment the conversation needs one. We do not build bots that pretend to be humans.
What does it cost to run each month?
Your monthly Stromation fee covers monitoring, support, reporting and minor changes. Third-party usage such as text messaging, hosting and AI processing is billed separately at cost, typically modest for a single location, and we estimate it for you up front.
What happens if the automation breaks?
Production workflows have error monitoring and alerting. Failures page us, not you. If something cannot be fixed immediately, leads route to a manual fallback so nothing is silently lost. Known limitations are documented, not hidden.
Do I own the system?
The workflows installed for you are documented and exportable. If you leave Stromation, you receive your workflow exports, documentation and data. Third-party accounts, such as your phone number and CRM, were always yours.
Can the AI make quotes or promises to my customers?
No. The AI collects information, answers basic questions from approved content and routes conversations. Pricing, scheduling commitments beyond approved booking rules, and anything sensitive go to your team.
