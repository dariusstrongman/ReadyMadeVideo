# Customer Offboarding

Leaving must be simple and complete. It is a selling point; honor it.

1. Confirm cancellation per agreement terms; schedule the cutover date.
2. Export the customer workflow JSONs + documentation; deliver with their data
   (the CRM was always theirs; include any Stromation-held records).
3. Deactivate their workflows on the cutover date. Do not delete for 30 days; then delete.
4. Rotate or revoke every credential they granted; confirm no exported copy contains secrets.
5. Remove tenant config. Keep the suppression list archived (the legal obligation survives).
6. Final invoice or settlement; confirm no further charges.
7. Exit note: reason recorded honestly in the CRM for churn analysis; door left open.
