get paid faster

Automate your invoicing so you get paid faster.

By Stromation · updated July 2026 · practical guide

Invoicing is the part of a one-person business that is boring, easy to put off, and directly tied to whether you get paid. Every day an invoice does not go out is a day money does not come in. So you take yourself out of the loop and let it run.

What gets automated

  • Creating the invoice: when a project is marked done (a form, a tag, a Stripe event), the invoice is generated with the right amount and line items.
  • Sending it: it goes to the client instantly, with a card-payment link built in.
  • Chasing it: if it is still unpaid after a few days, a polite reminder goes out on its own.

The flow

  1. Trigger: a webhook or schedule in n8n fires when work is complete.
  2. Build: it assembles the invoice and a Stripe payment link.
  3. Send: the client gets a clean email they can pay in two clicks.
  4. Follow up: unpaid after N days, a reminder sends automatically until it clears.

Why it matters: faster invoices get paid faster, and automatic reminders quietly recover the ones that would have slipped. You never again lose a Friday to catching up on billing.

Pair this with automatic payment recovery and almost nothing falls through.
