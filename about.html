about stromation

Reliability is the product.

Stromation exists because most small businesses do not need more AI hype. They need systems that answer the phone, follow up, and keep working when nobody is watching.

the founder

Darius Stroman

Darius is a North Texas technology professional with more than six years of enterprise IT and data-center operations experience: Linux systems, networking, server management, out-of-band management with iLO, iDRAC and IPMI, structured cabling and fiber, incident response, and high-availability environments where downtime is measured in dollars per minute.

He holds a Master of Science in Software Engineering, a bachelor's degree in Data Analytics, and the AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner certification, and builds with Python, SQL, Git, Docker, Kubernetes, n8n and modern AI tools.

Stromation is the product of that background applied to a problem he kept seeing: local service businesses losing real revenue to missed calls and slow follow-up, while being sold either overpriced enterprise software or AI magic that falls over in a week.

how we operate

The difference between hype and dependable automation.

documentation

Everything is written down

Every workflow ships with documentation: what it does, what it needs, how it fails, how to recover. If it is not documented, it is not done. That habit comes from data-center operations, where undocumented systems hurt people at 2am.

human authority

AI has a lane

Automation collects, routes and reports. Humans keep authority over pricing, commitments, emergencies and anything sensitive. We think that boundary is why our systems can be trusted with your customers.

monitoring

Failures alert us, not your customers

Every production workflow is monitored. When something breaks, Stromation gets paged and leads fall back to a manual path. Silent failure is the one thing an automation is never allowed to do.

honesty

No invented proof

No fake testimonials, no fabricated statistics, no stock-photo staff. Demonstrations are labeled as demonstrations. Results get published when real customers approve real numbers.

local

North Texas, on purpose.

Stromation serves Aubrey, Little Elm, Oak Point, Cross Roads, Denton, Prosper, Frisco, McKinney, The Colony, Lewisville, Carrollton, Plano and the broader Dallas-Fort Worth area. Local means we can sit across the table from you, and our reputation lives where you do.
