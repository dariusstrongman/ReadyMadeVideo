About Stromation

An AI-first video production platform in early development.

⚠ Stromation is a prototype. The AI editing pipeline is under active development. The current build demonstrates upload, structured timeline, and MP4 export. Automated professional editing is not yet live.

What Stromation is building

Stromation is designed around a single workflow: upload raw footage, describe the finished video you want, receive a structured first edit, and refine it through a browser-based studio. Every feature serves that sequence.

The long-term goal is to reduce the time between shooting and publishing — without requiring professional editing skills or a large production budget.

Who is building it

Darius Stroman is the founder and developer. He holds a Master of Science in Software Engineering, a bachelor's degree in Data Analytics, and the AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner certification. He has more than six years of enterprise IT and systems experience, and builds with Python, TypeScript, SQL, Docker, and modern AI tools.

Stromation is a solo project in early access. There is no team, no investors, and no launch date. The product ships when it is ready.

Current status

The current prototype demonstrates:

  • Authenticated user accounts via Manus OAuth
  • Private project creation and ownership
  • File upload to private S3 storage (user-scoped, signed-URL access)
  • Structured timeline data model (clips, tracks, in/out points)
  • Timeline viewer and basic editor interface
  • MP4 export from structured timeline data (Project Zero)

Automated AI editing, scene detection, and AI-driven clip selection are prototypes and are clearly labeled throughout the product.
