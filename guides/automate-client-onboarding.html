first impression

Automate client onboarding so it runs itself.

By Stromation · updated July 2026 · practical guide

The moment someone pays or signs is the moment your reputation is made or lost. A slow, clunky start makes even great work feel amateur. A sharp, instant welcome makes a one-person shop feel like a real company. The difference is a workflow.

What runs itself

When a new client comes in, the whole onboarding should happen without you: the welcome, the intake, the setup, the first meeting. You find out you have a new client because everything already handled itself.

The flow

  1. Trigger: a new payment (Stripe) or a signed proposal hits a webhook in n8n.
  2. Welcome: a branded welcome email goes out with a short intake questionnaire.
  3. Set up: their folder or workspace is created and they are logged in your tracker.
  4. Book: the kickoff call is scheduled automatically.

Every client gets the same sharp first experience, and you get hours back per client, plus you never forget a step when you are busy.

Why it matters more than it sounds: onboarding is where churn and refunds start. A confident, instant start sets the tone for the whole relationship.
