Common questions

When will pricing be announced?

We will announce pricing when the core product is ready for public launch. Early access members will be notified first and will receive founding pricing.

Will there be a free trial?

We plan to offer a way to try Stromation before committing to a paid plan. Details will be announced with the public pricing release.

What happens to my footage if I cancel?

You will have the ability to export your projects and delete all associated footage before cancelling. We will not retain your files after deletion is confirmed.

Is there a limit on footage file size?

We are designing the upload system to handle large raw video files, including 4K drone and camera footage. Specific limits will be defined per plan tier.

Will Stromation work for professional editors?

Stromation is designed to accelerate the editing workflow, not replace professional editors. The timeline and AI tools are built to complement existing skills and speed up the most time-consuming parts of the process.
