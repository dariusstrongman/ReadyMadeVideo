n8n vs Zapier vs Make: which should a solopreneur use?

By Stromation · updated July 2026 · practical guide

If you are automating a one-person business, the tool you pick matters more than any single workflow, because it sets your ceiling on cost and control. Here is the honest comparison for a solo operator in 2026.

Prices are approximate as of 2026 and change often, so check each site for the current number. The part that does not change is the billing model: per-task and per-operation meters charge you more the busier you get, while per-execution and self-hosting stay flat.

The honest take

Zapier is the easiest to start and fine for a handful of simple zaps. But its per-task pricing turns into a tax the moment your automations get busy, and you never own anything. Make is cheaper and more flexible, still metered and rented.

n8n wins for a solopreneur running lots of automations: self-host it for free or use a flat cloud plan, drop in real code and AI, and export the workflows so they are yours forever. No per-task meter punishing you for succeeding. The tradeoff is a slightly steeper start, which good templates erase.

Bottom line: if you will run more than a few automations, own the machine instead of renting access. That is why everything Stromation ships runs on n8n.
