questions

    
Straight answers.

    

      
Is the Starter Pack really free?
Yes. No card, no catch. Drop your email and we send you all five automations to keep forever. New ones come free as they're built.

      
Do I need to be technical?
No. Each automation is a step-by-step blueprint on n8n — if you can follow a recipe, you can run it. Most take under an hour to set up, and you can hand them to anyone.

      
What is n8n?
A free, open-source automation tool — like Zapier, but you own it and it doesn't charge you per task. Everything here runs on it, so you're never locked into a subscription just to keep your systems alive.

      
Do you build it for me, or do I?
The Starter Pack is do-it-yourself blueprints you plug in. Deeper, done-for-you systems and template packs come next — subscribers hear first.

      
Will these work with the tools I already use?
Yes — they connect to the tools you're already on: Gmail, Stripe, Google Sheets, your CRM, your calendar, and more. You wire them to your accounts and they run.

      
What happens after I get the pack?
You get new plug-and-play automations as they drop — the whole library over time, free. No spam, and one-click unsubscribe on every email.
