stop the leak

Recover failed payments automatically.

By Stromation · updated July 2026 · practical guide

If you sell anything on a subscription or send invoices, some payments fail. Cards expire, funds bounce, invoices get forgotten. For a solo business that money just quietly evaporates, because chasing it by hand is nobody's favorite job. So you automate the chase.

The two leaks this plugs

  • Failed charges: a subscription payment fails and, if nothing recovers it, that customer silently churns.
  • Overdue invoices: an invoice sits unpaid because no one followed up.

The flow

  1. Trigger: a daily schedule in n8n checks Stripe for failed charges and open, overdue invoices.
  2. Prioritize: it builds one list, biggest amounts first, so you see exactly what is at risk.
  3. Recover: optionally, it has Stripe re-send the invoice or a polite payment reminder automatically.
  4. Report: you get one email: total outstanding, and what got chased.

Why it pays for itself instantly: one recovered subscription or invoice usually dwarfs the cost of running the automation. This is money you already earned, just slipping through the cracks.
