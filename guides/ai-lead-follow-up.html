speed wins

AI lead follow-up: reply to every lead in minutes.

By Stromation · updated July 2026 · practical guide

Speed is the whole game with leads. Studies of inbound sales have shown for years that the business that responds first usually wins, and that the odds of reaching a lead fall off a cliff after the first few minutes. For a solo operator juggling the actual work, replying that fast by hand is impossible. So you automate it.

What "AI lead follow-up" actually means

It is a workflow that fires the instant a lead arrives from any source, works out who they are and what they want, and sends a personal-feeling first reply in minutes, then nudges you if they go quiet. Not a canned auto-responder, a real reply written in your voice.

The flow

  1. Trigger: a new form submission, inbound email, or DM hits a webhook in n8n.
  2. Enrich: a quick lookup fills in name, company, and any context you have.
  3. Draft: Claude writes a short first reply in your voice, referencing what they asked about.
  4. Send: it goes out, they land in your CRM, and a 48-hour reminder is set if they do not reply.

You choose the safety level: draft mode emails the reply to you to send with one tap, or auto mode sends it straight to the lead. Start in draft, switch to auto once you trust it.

Why it pays: most solo businesses lose deals not to price but to silence. Answering every lead in minutes, day or night, recovers the ones that used to slip.
