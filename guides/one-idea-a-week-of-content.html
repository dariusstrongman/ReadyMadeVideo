One idea into a week of content.

By Stromation · updated July 2026 · practical guide

Showing up online is how a one-person business gets found, but feeding the content machine by hand is a second full-time job. The fix is not posting less, it is turning one idea into a week of posts automatically, then approving them.

The idea: multiply, do not create from scratch

You have ideas all day. The bottleneck is turning each one into platform-ready posts. An AI content workflow does exactly that: you drop in one idea, and it returns a week of posts, each shaped for its platform, ready to schedule.

The flow

  1. Trigger: you add one idea to a sheet (or it pulls from your notes).
  2. Expand: Claude turns it into five to seven posts, each tuned to a platform (short hook-driven ones for X and TikTok, a longer take for LinkedIn, a caption for Instagram).
  3. Package: each gets a scroll-stopping hook, the body, and hashtags where they fit.
  4. Queue: they schedule out across the week, hands-off.

You stay in control of quality: skim the batch, tweak anything, and let it publish. One good idea becomes a week of presence in a few minutes.

Rule that keeps it good: feed it a real, specific idea, not a vague topic. Specific in, specific out. The AI multiplies your thinking, it does not replace it.
