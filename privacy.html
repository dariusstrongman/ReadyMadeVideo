Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 31, 2026

⚠ This policy describes the actual current implementation. Where a feature is not yet built, this is stated explicitly. This document will be updated as the product evolves.

1. What Stromation is

Stromation is an early-access video project tool. Authenticated users can upload video footage, structure a timeline, and download an edited video. AI-assisted features are prototypes and are clearly labelled throughout the product.

2. Authentication

Authentication is handled by Manus OAuth. When you sign in, we receive your Manus identity (name, email, and open ID). We store this in our database to associate your projects with your account. We do not store passwords. Manus’s own privacy policy governs the OAuth interaction.

3. Data we collect

We collect:

  • Your Manus OAuth identity (name, email, open ID)
  • Project metadata you create (title, description, category)
  • References to files you upload (storage key, file name, size, MIME type)
  • Timeline version data (structured JSON describing clip arrangement)
  • Processing job records (export status, render duration)

We do not collect payment information.

4. Your footage

Files you upload are stored in a private S3 bucket operated by Manus. Each file is stored under a path scoped to your user ID: users/{your_user_id}/projects/{project_id}/{filename}. No other user can access your files.

Access to stored files is provided only via time-limited signed URLs generated by the Manus storage proxy. Files are never publicly accessible by direct URL. Signed URLs expire after approximately one hour.

What we do not currently have: per-project access logs. Access logging is planned but not yet implemented. We cannot currently provide a history of who accessed a specific file.

5. External services that may receive your data

ServiceWhat it receivesPurpose
Manus OAuthLogin sessionAuthentication
Manus S3 storageUploaded footage, export filesFile storage
Google Analytics (GA4)Page views, anonymized usage eventsUsage analytics

Prototype AI features (scene detection, clip analysis) are not yet connected to any external inference API. When they are, this table will be updated before the feature is enabled.

6. AI processing

AI-assisted features are clearly labelled “prototype” throughout the product. We do not use your footage to train AI models without your explicit written consent. No footage is currently sent to any AI inference service. This policy will be updated before any AI feature that processes footage leaves prototype status.

7. Data retention and deletion

You can delete any project from your dashboard at any time. Deleting a project removes the project record and all associated metadata from our database. The underlying storage object is scheduled for deletion; deletion is not instantaneous and may take up to 24 hours to propagate through the storage system.

We do not retain footage after a deletion request is confirmed. If you wish to delete your account entirely, contact us and we will remove all data associated with your user ID within 30 days.

8. Encryption

Data in transit is encrypted via HTTPS (TLS). Storage-at-rest encryption is managed by the Manus storage provider. We do not configure or control the specific encryption algorithm used at rest; we rely on the provider’s default settings.

9. Cookies and analytics

We use Google Analytics (GA4) to understand aggregate usage patterns. GA4 sets cookies in your browser. No footage or personally identifiable project data is sent to Google Analytics. You can opt out using standard browser controls or a GA4 opt-out extension.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the project owner via the Manus platform or via the early access form.
