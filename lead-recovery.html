is this for you

Built for service businesses that live on inbound calls.

Industry call data says barely half of callers to home-services businesses reach a live person, and almost nobody leaves a voicemail (Invoca, 2026). If your trucks are busy, this is the profile the system was built for.

good fit
  • North Texas home-service company with roughly 2 to 25 employees
  • Leads arrive by phone, website forms, ads or referrals
  • Jobs are worth enough that one saved lead matters
  • You use a phone system, calendar, spreadsheet, CRM or field-service app
  • Someone on your team can approve messages and handle handoffs
not a fit yet
  • No consistent inbound lead volume to recover
  • Medical, legal-emergency or heavily regulated intake
  • Nobody available to take handoffs during business hours
  • Expecting a fully autonomous AI receptionist with no human involvement

If that last one is you: we deliberately do not sell that. Controlled automation with human authority is the standard here.

what gets installed

The system components.

missed-call response

Instant text-back

Verified missed-call events trigger an approved, branded response within seconds. Duplicate events are detected and ignored. Opt-outs are honored immediately and permanently.

qualification

Structured intake

AI collects name, service need, location and urgency into a structured record. It cannot quote prices, make commitments or give safety advice. Low confidence or sensitive topics route to your team.

crm + booking

Clean records and scheduling

The system searches before creating contacts, prevents duplicates, attaches the conversation summary and source, and offers booking through your calendar where appropriate.

monitoring

Error handling and reporting

Failures alert Stromation, not your customers. Leads fall back to a manual queue rather than vanishing. You get a weekly report of calls, responses, qualified leads and bookings.

security and control

Boring, documented, reversible. On purpose.

how credentials are handled
  • Your account credentials stay in encrypted credential storage, never in workflow files or documents
  • Each integration gets only the minimum access it needs
  • Access is revocable by you at any time; offboarding includes credential rotation
messaging compliance
  • Business texting in the US requires carrier registration (A2P 10DLC). We handle the registration process with you before launch
  • STOP and HELP keywords are processed immediately and permanently
  • Consent and opt-out records are kept and honored across every workflow

Compliance settings are configured per customer and documented. Regulatory requirements are confirmed against current carrier and provider rules during implementation.

questions

Common questions.

How fast does the text-back actually go out?
Typically within seconds of the missed-call event reaching the system. Exact timing depends on your phone provider's event delivery, which we test and measure during implementation.
What if a customer replies something the AI cannot handle?
The conversation escalates to your team with full context. The AI has a defined lane: collect information, answer approved basics, route. Everything else is human.
Can we turn it off?
Yes, instantly. Workflows can be paused at any time and leads route to a manual fallback. You also keep documented exports of your workflows if you leave.
Does this work after hours?
Yes, that is when it earns its keep. Response templates and booking rules can differ for after-hours, and quiet-hour rules for outbound messaging are respected.
What do you need from us?
Access to connect your phone system, website forms, calendar and CRM, someone to approve message templates, and a person who receives handoffs. The audit confirms the details.
