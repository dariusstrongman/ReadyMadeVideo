The problem

Editing is where great footage goes to die

Creators spend more time organizing and editing than they do actually creating. The gap between raw footage and a finished video is enormous.

📁

Hours of organization

Sorting, naming, and reviewing hundreds of clips before a single cut is made.

✂

Blank timeline paralysis

Staring at an empty timeline with no clear starting point or structure.

🔄

Endless revision cycles

Rebuilding edits from scratch every time the direction changes.

🎵

Audio and music sync

Finding the right music, syncing it, and balancing levels takes hours alone.

📱

Multi-platform repurposing

Reformatting the same content for YouTube, Instagram, and TikTok separately.

⏱

Time that kills consistency

The editing bottleneck is the main reason creators post less than they want to.

How it works

From raw footage to finished video in five steps

01

Upload everything

Drag and drop your phone, camera, and drone footage. Large files upload directly to secure storage.

02

Describe your vision

Tell Stromation the tone, pace, platform, and style. Or use a detailed prompt for precise direction.

03

Stromation builds the edit

The AI analyzes every clip, selects the strongest moments, structures the story, and creates the timeline.

04

Refine anything

Accept the edit, adjust it manually in the studio, or tell the AI what to change in plain language.

05

Export everywhere

Export in 1080p or 4K, in any aspect ratio, with captions burned in or as a separate file.

The platform

Everything you need. Nothing you don’t.

Stromation is designed around one workflow: upload, describe, edit, export. Every feature serves that sequence.

Footage intelligence

Prototype

Every clip analyzed. The best moments surfaced.

Stromation examines each clip for shot type, camera movement, action, audio quality, and visual clarity.

Natural-language search — find clips by describing what you want: “drone shots where I’m running uphill.”

Your footage is yours

Built with footage privacy as a first principle

Your raw footage is personal. It is not shared, sold, or used to train AI models without your explicit permission.

🔒

Private by default

All footage is stored in private object storage. Files are accessible only via time-limited signed URLs — no permanent public URLs are generated for your raw files.

🚫

No training without consent

Your footage is not used to train AI models. Any future opt-in program will be clearly labeled and voluntary.

🗑

You control deletion

Delete any project and all associated records are removed from our database. Storage file deletion is scheduled on project delete. See the Privacy Policy for full details.

🔑

Short-lived access

Media is delivered via signed, time-limited URLs. Footage cannot be accessed without an active authenticated session.

🏢

Project isolation

Each project’s footage is isolated at the storage level. No cross-project access is possible.

📋

Access logging

Access logging is planned. The current prototype does not yet generate per-project access logs. This will be added before public launch.
